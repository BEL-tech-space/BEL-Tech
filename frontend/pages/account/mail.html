Dear BELTechies,

Welcome to BEL Tech! We’re excited to have you join our growing community of eco-conscious individuals making a positive impact on the planet.

With BEL Tech, recycling is not just easy – it’s rewarding! Here’s how you can get started:

Collect Your Recyclables: Gather items like plastic bottles, cans, or other approved waste materials. Visit a Recycling Station: Drop off your recyclables with one of our authorized collectors. Earn BEL Tokens: Use the app to connect with the collector, and receive BEL tokens instantly in your wallet. Redeem and Track: Use your tokens for rewards or track your recycling progress right in the app. We’re here to support you every step of the way. If you have any questions, feel free to contact us at support@beltech.site or visit our FAQ section in the app.

Together, let’s build a cleaner, greener future!

Happy Recycling, The BEL Tech Team
